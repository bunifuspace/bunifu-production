About us

We started with a simple idea.

Make good work. Make it matter. And make sure it lasts.

Mwanza, Tanzania

Where it began

Before the signs, there was a vision.

City Signs began in Mwanza with a belief that a business should be recognizable before anyone walks through its doors.

What started as a small creative operation grew through years of listening to businesses, understanding their ambitions and learning what it takes to turn an idea into something people can actually see, touch and remember.

We didn't want to be another company that simply produced what was ordered. We wanted to understand the story behind the work — then help give that story a physical presence.

The journey

Built one project at a time.

2016

The beginning

City Signs takes its first steps in Mwanza, focused on creative production and helping local businesses show up with confidence.

2018

Finding our shape

Printing, signage, branding and fabrication begin coming together, allowing projects to move from an idea to a finished result under one roof.

2020

Beyond the sign

Our work expands into corporate environments, retail spaces, institutions, apparel, large-format production and branded experiences.

Today

Still making visible

We continue to grow with the businesses we serve, bringing creative thinking and physical production together for work made to be seen.

What we believe

Good design gets attention. Great execution keeps it.

A beautiful concept means very little if it cannot survive production, installation, distance, weather, time or everyday use. That's why we care about both sides of the work — the creative thought and the physical reality.

Our difference 06

We don't separate the idea from the making.

That's the difference. The person designing your sign can speak to the person producing it. The people planning the installation understand what was designed. Every stage can respond to the one before it.

Fewer gaps. Better decisions. More control over the final result.

From Mwanza

Mwanza is where our story began, and it continues to shape how we work. But good ideas don't stop at city limits. We work with businesses and institutions across Tanzania, carrying the same attention to detail wherever the project takes us.
