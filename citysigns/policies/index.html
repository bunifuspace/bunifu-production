Policies

Policies

Clear terms.
Good business.

We believe good working relationships begin with clarity. These policies explain how City Signs handles projects, information, payments, materials and communication.

01 02 03 04 05 06
Our approach

Nothing hidden.
Nothing complicated.

From quotation to delivery, we aim to make every stage of a project straightforward. If something needs clarification, we would rather discuss it early than leave room for assumptions.

01
Projects & quotations

Every project starts with a clear brief.

Quotations are prepared based on the information, specifications, quantities, materials and production requirements provided by the client. Changes to the brief may affect the final cost and delivery timeline.

A quotation does not automatically constitute an order. Production begins after the required approval and payment or deposit have been confirmed.

02
Design & approval

We make it right before production.

Artwork, dimensions, colours, wording and other project details should be reviewed and approved by the client before production.

Once final artwork or specifications have been approved, changes requested after production has started may result in additional charges and revised delivery dates.

03
Payments

Good work needs a clear commitment.

Payment terms vary depending on the size and nature of the project. Deposits may be required before materials are purchased or production begins.

Final balances are payable according to the agreed terms. Completed work may be withheld from collection or delivery where outstanding payments remain due.

04
Production & materials

Materials matter. So does the craft.

We select production methods and materials according to the agreed specifications and intended application. Where exact material availability changes, we may recommend a suitable alternative for approval.

Minor variations in colour, texture, finish or material can occur between digital previews, samples and finished production.

05
Installation & delivery

From our workshop to your space.

Installation requirements, access conditions, site preparation and working hours should be communicated before installation is scheduled.

Additional work caused by unforeseen site conditions, restricted access, structural issues or changes requested on site may be charged separately.

06
Privacy & information

Your information stays respected.

Information provided to City Signs is used to communicate with clients, prepare quotations, fulfil orders, coordinate projects and provide customer support.

We do not intentionally sell client information to third parties. Information may be shared with trusted service providers where necessary to complete a project or fulfil a requested service.

A final word

If something isn't
clear, ask us.

These policies are designed to establish a practical foundation for working together. Specific projects may have additional terms agreed between City Signs and the client.
