Terms

Terms and Conditions

Clear terms.
Better work.

Every good project needs a shared understanding. These terms define how City Signs and our clients work together, from the first quotation to final delivery.

CS
Working together

A project is more than
a transaction. It's a process.

We work with clients across branding, printing, signage, fabrication, installation and creative production. These terms provide the framework for that work while allowing each project to have its own requirements.

01
Acceptance

Starting a project means agreeing.

By approving a quotation, making a payment, confirming production, supplying artwork for production, or otherwise instructing City Signs to proceed, the client accepts these terms together with any project-specific terms agreed in writing.

02
Quotations

Every number has a reason.

Quotations are prepared according to the information available at the time. They may include design, materials, production, transport, installation or other agreed services.

Unless otherwise stated, quotations may be subject to change where project specifications, quantities, materials or requirements change.

03
Orders

Approval moves the project forward.

Production begins after the client has approved the relevant quotation, artwork, specifications and payment requirements.

Verbal instructions may be accepted where appropriate, but important project changes should be confirmed through a written communication.

04
Artwork

Approved means ready for production.

Clients are responsible for reviewing names, spelling, measurements, colours, logos, images and other supplied information before approving artwork.

City Signs is not responsible for errors contained in client-approved artwork or specifications.

05
Client materials

Your files. Your responsibility.

Clients must have the necessary rights, permissions and licences to use logos, photographs, artwork, fonts, trademarks and other materials supplied to City Signs.

The client remains responsible for claims arising from materials it provides to us.

06
Payment

Production follows the commitment.

Deposits and advance payments may be required depending on the nature and value of a project. Payment schedules are communicated in the quotation or project agreement.

Outstanding balances may delay delivery, installation or collection until payment has been received.

07
Changes and cancellations

Changes have a cost.

Changes requested after approval may require additional design, material or production costs. They may also affect the agreed completion date.

If a project is cancelled after production or procurement has begun, the client may be responsible for costs already incurred.

08
Production

Craft has natural variation.

Printed colours, textures, finishes and materials may vary slightly between digital previews, samples and finished products. Such variations do not automatically constitute a production defect.

Manufacturing tolerances may also apply to dimensions, cutting, stitching, fabrication and installation.

09
Delivery

Good work should arrive properly.

Delivery and installation dates are estimates unless a specific date has been expressly agreed. Delays caused by circumstances outside our reasonable control may affect completion.

The client is responsible for providing suitable access and any necessary permissions for delivery or installation.

10
Ownership

The finished work is yours. The process remains ours.

Unless otherwise agreed, final physical products become the client's property once all amounts due have been paid.

Design concepts, unused concepts, working files, production methods and internal materials remain the property of City Signs unless specifically transferred through a separate agreement.

11
Portfolio

Good work deserves to be seen.

Unless a client requests confidentiality in writing, City Signs may photograph completed work and use those images for portfolio, marketing and promotional purposes.

Confidential or unreleased projects will not knowingly be published before the agreed release date.

12
Responsibility

We take responsibility for our part.

City Signs will make reasonable efforts to deliver services according to the agreed specifications and professional standards.

We are not responsible for losses caused by inaccurate client information, unauthorised changes, unsuitable client-supplied materials, site conditions or circumstances beyond our reasonable control.
