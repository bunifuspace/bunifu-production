What We Believe

We believe in a God whose love never fails, even in a broken world. Despite the cosmic conflict that has raged since before time, God’s plan of salvation continues. Jesus is coming soon to restore our world to its original perfection. We await with joy the day when God will give us new hearts in a new earth, where we’ll live in His presence for eternity.

Identity

We are a movement of believers united by our love for Jesus and our commitment to His Word. Our identity is rooted in the Bible’s call to be God’s remnant people—sharing His final message of mercy with a world that desperately needs hope. As Seventh-day Adventists, we embrace Scripture as our foundation, Jesus as our Savior, and the Sabbath as our weekly reminder of God’s creative and redemptive power. We are not just a denomination but a worldwide family, diverse in culture yet united in mission.

Learn More

Belief

Our beliefs are built on Jesus Christ—the Author and Finisher of our faith. Scripture reveals God’s character and His plan for humanity. From Creation to the Second Coming, we see God’s loving pursuit of His children. We embrace the whole Bible, allowing Scripture to interpret itself. Our distinctive understandings of prophecy, the sanctuary, and the state of the dead provide clarity in a confused world. But above all, we believe in grace—God’s gift that transforms lives and prepares hearts for eternity. Our doctrines aren’t mere theological positions; they’re invitations to experience God’s transforming love.

Learn More

Impact

Christ’s love compels us to be His hands and feet in a hurting world. Through education, healthcare, disaster relief, and community development, we demonstrate the gospel in action. Our network of over 8,000 schools educates millions, while our hospitals bring healing to communities across six continents. Through ADRA (Adventist Development and Relief Agency), we respond to crises and develop lasting solutions for vulnerable communities. Whether teaching children to read, providing clean water to villages, or offering spiritual support during life’s darkest moments, we believe service is worship. Join us in making an eternal difference.
