# WHO WE ARE

## A Community of Faith, Hope, and Love

Buzuruga Seventh-day Adventist Church is a community of believers united by faith in Jesus Christ and grounded in the teachings of the Bible.

We are part of the worldwide Seventh-day Adventist Church, a global movement called to share the good news of God's love with people of every nation, language, and culture.

Here in Buzuruga, we are a family committed to growing together in faith, supporting one another, serving our community, and preparing for the soon return of Jesus Christ.

We believe church is more than a place to gather each Sabbath. It is a community where people can belong, ask questions, find encouragement, and experience the transforming love of God.

---

# WHAT WE BELIEVE

## Rooted in Scripture. Centered on Jesus.

Our faith is built on the Bible and centered on Jesus Christ.

We believe that God loves every person and desires a personal relationship with each of us. Even in a world marked by pain, uncertainty, and brokenness, God's plan of salvation offers hope.

We believe Jesus Christ came to save humanity through His life, death, and resurrection. Through Him, we find forgiveness, freedom, healing, and new life.

We look forward to the promised return of Jesus and the restoration of all things. Until that day, we choose to live with faith, hope, and love.

Our beliefs are not simply ideas to understand. They are truths that shape how we live, how we serve, and how we see the world.

---

# WHAT WE DO

## Faith in Action

At Buzuruga Seventh-day Adventist Church, faith goes beyond the walls of our church.

We gather to worship, study the Bible, pray, and grow together. We also believe that following Jesus means caring for people and serving the community around us.

Through worship, outreach, youth ministry, children's ministry, community service, health initiatives, and other activities, we seek practical ways to share God's love.

Every act of kindness, every prayer, every conversation, and every opportunity to serve is a chance to reflect the character of Christ.

We are committed to being a church that welcomes, serves, and makes a positive difference in the lives of others.

# OUR IDENTITY

## A Global Faith. A Local Family.

Buzuruga Seventh-day Adventist Church is part of a worldwide family of believers united by faith in Jesus Christ and commitment to God's Word.

Our identity is rooted in the Bible, shaped by the life and teachings of Jesus, and guided by the hope of His soon return.

As Seventh-day Adventists, we believe in the authority of Scripture, salvation through Jesus Christ, the importance of the Sabbath, and God's promise to restore the world.

But our identity is also expressed in how we live.

We are called to love our neighbors, care for those in need, grow in faith, and share the hope we have found in Christ.

We may come from different backgrounds and experiences, but together we are one family with one mission: to know Jesus and make Him known.

# OUR BELIEFS

## Faith That Shapes Life

Our beliefs begin and end with Jesus Christ.

The Bible reveals who God is, His love for humanity, and His plan to restore what sin has broken.

From Creation to the promise of a new earth, Scripture tells the story of a God who continually reaches out to His people.

We believe the Bible is God's Word and a guide for faith and daily living. Through Scripture, we discover God's grace, understand His purpose, and learn how to live with hope.

Our beliefs are more than teachings to memorize.

They are an invitation to know God personally, experience His transforming grace, and live with confidence in His promises.
