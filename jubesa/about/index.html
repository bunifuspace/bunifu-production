About Jubesa

Built around
possibility.

We bring agriculture, natural resources and trade together with a long-term view of what Tanzania can produce, develop and become.

Who we are

More than
one business.

Jubesa Farm Tanzania Limited is a Tanzanian company built around productive opportunity.

Our activities span agriculture, natural resources and trade — three areas connected by a simple idea: create value from what Tanzania has to offer.

From the fields we cultivate to the resources beneath the surface and the products moving through our markets, we look at each opportunity as part of something larger.

Our perspective

Think beyond
today.

We believe meaningful businesses are built over time. That means looking beyond immediate activity and considering the people, resources, markets and opportunities that make long-term growth possible.

01

Productive

We focus on activities that turn resources, ideas and opportunities into measurable value.

02

Responsible

We approach growth with respect for resources, people and the environments in which we operate.

03

Long-term

We build with the future in mind, favouring durable opportunities over short-term thinking.

04

Connected

We see agriculture, resources and trade as connected parts of a wider economic ecosystem.

Our vision

A more productive
Tanzania.

We see a Tanzania with greater capacity to transform its agricultural potential, natural resources and commercial opportunities into lasting value.

Our mission

Turn opportunity
into value.

Our mission is to build and operate businesses that make productive use of available opportunities while creating value for customers, partners, communities and the wider economy.

Looking ahead

There is more
to build.

Jubesa is built with room to grow. As new opportunities emerge, we intend to keep expanding our ability to produce, develop, connect and create value.
