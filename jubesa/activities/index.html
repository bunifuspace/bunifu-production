What we do

Diverse industries.
Connected opportunities.

Jubesa Farm Tanzania Limited operates across a diverse range of commercial sectors, bringing together agricultural production, natural resources, general supply, textiles, electronics, hardware, construction materials and trade logistics. Our activities are structured around identifying market needs, connecting customers with suitable products and resources, and developing business relationships across different industries.

From agricultural markets and institutional procurement to industrial supplies and construction-related requirements, our business scope reflects an adaptable approach to sourcing, supplying and distributing products for individual, commercial and organizational needs.
