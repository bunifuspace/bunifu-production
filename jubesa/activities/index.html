Business Activities

What we do

Building value
across opportunities.

Jubesa Farm Tanzania Limited operates across agriculture, natural resources and trade, bringing together productive activities that create value from Tanzania's land, resources and markets.

Our activities

Different industries.
One long-term view.

01

Agriculture

Agricultural production across a diverse range of crops, with a focus on productive land use, efficient operations and opportunities across agricultural markets.

  • Crop production
  • Farm operations
  • Agricultural development
  • Market-oriented production
02

Natural Resources

Exploring opportunities within Tanzania's natural resource base, with an emphasis on responsible development and long-term economic value.

  • Mineral resources
  • Resource exploration
  • Resource development
  • Value creation
03

Enterprise

Connecting products and opportunities with markets through commercial activities that support the movement of goods from source to destination.

  • Agricultural products
  • Commercial sourcing
  • Market connections
  • Domestic and international opportunities
