Business Activities

What we do

Building value
across opportunities.

Jubesa Farm Tanzania Limited operates across agriculture, natural resources, general supply, textiles, electronics, construction materials and delivery services. We connect products, resources and markets through diverse commercial activities designed to meet business and institutional needs.

Our activities

Diverse services.
One business partner.

01

Agriculture

Agricultural production and farm development focused on productive land use, efficient operations and opportunities across agricultural markets.

  • Crop production
  • Farm operations
  • Agricultural development
  • Agricultural products
02

Natural Resources

Exploring opportunities within Tanzania's natural resource sector, with an emphasis on responsible development and long-term value.

  • Mineral resources
  • Resource exploration
  • Resource development
  • Value creation
03

General Supply

Supplying a broad range of essential products to businesses, institutions and other customers.

  • Foodstuffs and consumables
  • Educational materials
  • Office and stationery supplies
  • General merchandise
04

Textile Products

Providing textile products and fabric-related goods for individual, commercial and institutional needs.

  • Fabrics and textiles
  • Clothing and garments
  • Uniforms and workwear
  • Textile sourcing and supply
05

Electronics and Hardware

Supplying electronic equipment, hardware products and building materials to support homes, businesses and construction projects.

  • Electronic equipment and accessories
  • Electrical supplies
  • Hardware and tools
  • Building and construction materials
06

Trade and Delivery

Connecting suppliers and customers through commercial sourcing, product distribution and delivery services.

  • Commercial sourcing
  • Product distribution
  • Delivery services
  • Domestic and international trade opportunities
