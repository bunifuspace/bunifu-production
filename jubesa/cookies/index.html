This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how they may be used on the Jubesa Farm Tanzania Limited website and the choices available to visitors.

01

Understanding cookies

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device when you visit them. They allow a website to recognize a device and remember certain information between visits.

Cookies can support website functionality, improve performance and help website operators understand how visitors interact with their websites.

02

How we use them

How cookies may be used

Cookies may be used on this website to support essential functionality, remember preferences, improve performance and provide a more consistent browsing experience.

Website functionality

Certain cookies may be necessary for parts of the website to operate correctly and securely.

Preferences

Cookies may allow a website to remember selected preferences and settings so that they do not need to be entered repeatedly.

Performance

Certain technical information may help us understand how the website performs and identify areas that could be improved.

03

Types

Types of cookies

Cookies can serve different purposes depending on how they are used.

Essential cookies

These cookies may be required for core website functionality, security or technical operations.

Preference cookies

These cookies can remember choices or settings made during previous visits.

Analytics cookies

Where analytics services are used, cookies may help provide aggregated information about website traffic and usage.

04

Third parties

Third-party cookies

Some services integrated into a website may place their own cookies or similar technologies on a visitor's device.

These may include external services used for website functionality, security, analytics or embedded content.

Third-party providers operate according to their own privacy and cookie policies, which may differ from those of Jubesa Farm Tanzania Limited.

05

Your choices

Managing cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to view, restrict, block or delete cookies through their settings.

You can choose to disable cookies, although doing so may affect the functionality or appearance of some websites.

Browser settings

Cookie controls are generally available within your browser's privacy, security or site settings.

06

Privacy

Cookies and personal information

Cookies do not necessarily identify you by name. Depending on how they are configured, however, information collected through cookies may potentially be associated with other information.

Our handling of personal information is described in our Privacy Policy.

07

Updates

Changes to this policy

This Cookies Policy may be updated when our website, technologies or business practices change.

Any revised version will be published on this page together with an updated date.
