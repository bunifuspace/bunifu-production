Regional trade

Trade that moves with opportunity.

Jubesa Farm Tanzania Limited extends its commercial activities into regional trade, importing selected pre-owned footwear, clothing and electronics from Nairobi, Kenya, for markets in Tanzania.

What we trade

Selected goods.
Real markets.

01

Pre-owned Footwear

Second-hand shoes sourced through regional trade channels, serving everyday demand across retail and commercial markets.

  • Second-hand shoes
  • Mixed footwear selections
  • Retail and resale opportunities
02

Pre-owned Clothing

Second-hand garments selected for practical use and commercial resale, connecting established supply with customers in Tanzania.

  • Second-hand clothing
  • Mixed garment selections
  • Retail and wholesale trade
03

Electronics

Pre-owned electronics sourced through regional commercial channels, supporting access to consumer and commercial goods.

  • Pre-owned electronics
  • Consumer devices
  • Commercial goods
