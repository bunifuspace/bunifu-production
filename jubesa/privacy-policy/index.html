Privacy Policy

Your privacy

Information handled
with care.

This Privacy Policy explains how Jubesa Farm Tanzania Limited handles information provided through this website and how we seek to protect the privacy of our visitors, customers and business contacts.

01

Information

Information we collect

We may receive information that you voluntarily provide when communicating with Jubesa Farm Tanzania Limited through this website or through other direct business communications.

Information you provide

This may include your name, email address, telephone number, company or organization, and any other information you choose to include in an enquiry or communication.

Technical information

Like many websites, basic technical information may be collected automatically by the website or its hosting infrastructure. This may include information such as browser type, device information, approximate location and general website usage data.

02

Use of information

How we use information

Information provided to us may be used to understand and respond to enquiries, communicate with visitors and support legitimate business activities.

Communication

We may use contact information to respond to requests, provide information about our activities or continue a business communication initiated by you.

Website improvement

General technical and usage information may be used to understand how the website performs and to improve its content, security and overall experience.

03

Protection

Protecting your information

We take reasonable measures to protect information entrusted to us against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure or loss.

However, no method of electronic transmission, website infrastructure or digital storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

04

Sharing

When information may be shared

Jubesa Farm Tanzania Limited does not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, support legitimate business operations, comply with applicable legal obligations or protect the rights and security of the company and others.

05

Third parties

External services and websites

Our website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties. These websites have their own privacy practices and policies.

Jubesa Farm Tanzania Limited is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of external websites. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of any third-party website they choose to visit.

06

Your choices

Your information and choices

You may choose what information you provide when communicating with us. If you have questions about information you have provided to Jubesa, you may contact us directly.

Where applicable, we will consider reasonable requests concerning access, correction or removal of personal information, subject to legal and legitimate business requirements.

07

Children

Children's privacy

This website is intended for general business and informational purposes. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through the website.

08

Updates

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to our website, business practices or legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective or update date.
