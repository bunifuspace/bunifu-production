    I start with idea.

    Discover

    What are we trying to say?

    I begin by understanding the story, the people, the purpose and the audience.

    Define

    What should people feel?

    I find the central idea and decide what the film needs to communicate.

    Create

    How should it look, sound and move?

    Shot planning. Locations. Interviews. Schedule. Equipment. Crew. Details.

    Capture
